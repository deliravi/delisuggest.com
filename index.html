FAQ

Questions people ask us

When does Delisuggest launch?

No fixed dates — we ship when it's ready. The closed beta is running with a small group; follow t.me/deliravi to hear about wider access first.

What does "format-independent" actually mean?

One feed holds documents, files, apps, regular media and platform-specific embeds like YouTube or Spotify. You don't switch between apps — the feed adapts to the format that fits your current need.

How is my data protected?

Registration asks for minimal data, and your recommendations are encrypted with a key that belongs to you — not to us. Ratings shared for community features are anonymized.

What is an AIPool?

A community-built feed recipe. Create your own with the AIPool Maker, or install pools from other users in the AIPool Market — full control, always.

Can my organization use it?

Yes — adaptive educational feeds for B2B are planned, with QR-based access and dynamic pricing. Reach out via Telegram or email to talk about a pilot.
